# 📖 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Here are answers to the most common questions regarding **Otis AI Companion**.

---

### 🔒 Is Otis AI Companion completely private and local-first?
**Yes.** All core features—including Area Discovery, Spatial Adjacency Graph mapping, Breadth-First Search pathfinding, SQLite habit pattern mining, speaker presence routing, and dashboard generation—run **100% locally** on your Home Assistant host without external dependencies. 

External cloud APIs (such as Google Gemini or OpenAI) are used **strictly** if you configure them for multimodal walkthrough video transcription or conversational chat queries.

---

### 💳 Does Otis AI consume lots of LLM API tokens?
**No.** Otis AI executes routine analysis and entity auditing using deterministic Python algorithms and local SQLite queries. External LLMs are queried only when you actively chat with Otis or upload a video walkthrough for multimodal audio transcription.

---

### 🗑️ Can I delete or customize the auto-generated dashboards?
**Yes.** All dashboards generated by Otis AI (including `/otis-ai` and domain command centers) are synchronized in real-time with Home Assistant's in-memory `DashboardsCollection` (`lovelace/dashboards/list`). 

You can edit, reorder, or delete any generated dashboard directly by navigating to **Settings → Dashboards** in Home Assistant.

---

### 🏢 How does Otis AI handle multi-floor homes?
Otis AI natively queries Home Assistant's **Floor Registry** (`core.floor_registry`). During spatial layout generation, it groups rooms into floors (`Ground Floor`, `Upper Level`, `Outdoor & Perimeter`), draws directional stairwell transition markers (`Back Stairs UP ↗ / DN ↙`), and renders floor isolation filter pills on the dashboard card.

---

### 🔋 Will sleeping battery sensors or offline devices get deleted as orphans?
**No.** Otis AI enforces an **Offline Device Protection Gate**. Entities tied to an active, installed integration (`config_entry_id`) are strictly preserved and never flagged as orphans, even if they are in setup retry, powered off, or sleeping to conserve battery.

---

### 🎨 Can I customize the colors of the Otis Companion card?
**Yes.** In addition to the 6 built-in presets (`ha_native`, `cyberpunk`, `midnight`, `emerald`, `sunset`, `light_frost`), the card is built with standard `<ha-card>` DOM elements and exposes `--otis-*` CSS custom properties, allowing full custom styling via `lovelace-card-mod`.
